General Information For The IP380
Island Packet 380
The Island Packet 380 represents a further evolution of the design, engineering and construction philosophy proven in over 1600 yachts and in countless miles of all kinds of cruising around the globe. With its modern Full Foil Keel, cutter rig, and impressive interior accommodation plan, the IP380 is yet another prime example of why Island Packet Yachts has become America's Cruising Yacht Leader.

- from the factory brochure
