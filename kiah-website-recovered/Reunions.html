About Reunions

Dave (Tiny) Brighton decided in the late 1990s to try to find as many of the Eagle 4th and 5th Commissions Communications lads. Our old mate worked hard and by late 2000 he had found sufficient of us to suggest a reunion. This was the first of several and took place in Weymouth in the year 2000. It was a great success and we had a wonderful time catching up with each other and sharing stories of our time on Eagle and our lives since then. We have had several more reunions since then, the most recent being in Dorchester/Weymouth in 2025. This 2025 one was of particular significance as we took the ashes of Florrie Ford, from Australia and Nobby Clarke from the UK and arranged to scatter their ashes off Portland Bill.
