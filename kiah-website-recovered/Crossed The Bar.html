Those Who Have Crossed The Bar

Crossed The Bar

"Sunset and evening star, and one clear call for me! And may there be no moaning of the bar, when I put out to sea."

This page honors the brave men of HMS Eagle who gave their lives in service, whether at sea or in the years that followed. They served with courage and dedication, and their memory lives on in the hearts of their shipmates and families.

We remember their sacrifice and the contributions they made during the Commission years of 1964 to 1967, and beyond. May they rest in peace.
